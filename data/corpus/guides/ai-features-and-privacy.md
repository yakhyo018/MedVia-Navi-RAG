# AI features and your privacy

MedVia uses AI in three places: translating chat messages, writing a Korean summary of your questionnaire for the doctor, and the booking assistant that finds clinics and free times for you. All three need your consent, which you can give or withdraw at any time under Settings → AI.

The booking assistant never books on its own: it proposes a time, and only your Confirm press holds the slot. It can be used up to 30 messages per day.

AI on MedVia does not diagnose and does not replace a doctor. For any medical question, ask the doctor at the clinic.
