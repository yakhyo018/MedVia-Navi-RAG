# The pre-visit questionnaire

When you send a booking request, MedVia asks four short questions: your main concern, the medications you take, any allergies, and previous treatments. You can answer in your own language.

## Who can see your answers

Your answers are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and shown only to the clinic and the doctor of that booking. MedVia staff and other clinics cannot read them. Every time the clinic or doctor opens your answers, it is recorded; you can see every reading under Privacy in your account.

## The Korean summary

If you agreed to AI processing in Settings, an AI writes a short Korean summary of your answers for the doctor. The summary only repeats what you wrote — it does not diagnose and does not recommend a treatment.
