# How booking works on MedVia

## Choosing a time

Every clinic page lists its treatments with a starting price and a duration. Times are offered in 30-minute slots, in Seoul time (KST, UTC+9). A 60-minute treatment uses two consecutive slots. You can book up to 60 days ahead, and a slot must start at least 3 hours from now so the clinic has time to answer.

## Holding a slot

When you pick a time, MedVia holds it for you for 10 minutes while you fill in the request. If you do not send the request within 10 minutes, the time is released for other patients. You can have at most 3 requests waiting for a clinic's answer at the same time.

## The clinic's answer

The clinic has 24 hours to confirm or decline your request, and must answer at least one hour before the visit. A request the clinic does not answer in time expires, and the time is released. You get a notification when the clinic confirms, declines, or the request expires.

## Reminders

MedVia sends a reminder 24 hours before a confirmed visit.
