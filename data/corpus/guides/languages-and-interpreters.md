# Languages and interpreters

MedVia is available in Korean, English, Russian, Uzbek, Kazakh and Chinese.

Each clinic page shows the languages its staff speak and the languages its interpreter covers. Clinics that list your language provide an interpreter at no extra charge. You can filter clinics by language and by "interpreter available" in the search.

## Chat with the clinic

After you book, you can chat with the clinic. If you agreed to AI processing, messages are translated automatically: you write in your language and the clinic reads Korean, and the other way round. The original message is always kept next to the translation.
