# Cancelling or changing a booking

You can cancel a booking yourself from My Bookings up to 24 hours before the visit. Within the last 24 hours the cancel button is no longer available; write to the clinic in the chat instead.

To change the time, cancel the booking (if more than 24 hours remain) and book a new time. There is no cancellation fee on MedVia, because MedVia takes no payments: you pay the clinic directly at the visit.

If you do not come to a confirmed visit, the clinic marks the booking as a no-show.
