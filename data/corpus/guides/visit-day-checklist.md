# Checklist for the day of your visit

- Check the time in My Bookings: all times on MedVia are Seoul time (KST).
- Arrive about 10 minutes early for registration.
- Bring your passport and the list of medications you take.
- Tell the clinic about allergies again at the desk, even if you wrote them in the questionnaire.
- Prices on MedVia are starting prices. The final price is set by the clinic after the consultation, and you pay the clinic directly at the visit.
- If you are late or cannot come, write to the clinic in the chat as early as possible.
